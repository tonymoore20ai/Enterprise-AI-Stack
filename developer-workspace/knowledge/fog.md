# Driving in fog

Source: https://www.weather.gov/safety/fog-driving

## Driving through fog
- Slow down and allow extra time for your journey.
- Use low-beam headlights, which also turn on your tail lights so others can see you. Use fog lights if the car has them. Never use high beams: they reflect off the fog and make visibility worse.
- Leave a much larger gap than usual to the vehicle ahead so you can stop suddenly.
- Use the lines on the road to keep your position in the lane.

## If visibility drops to near zero
- Turn on your hazard lights.
- Pull completely off the road into a safe place, such as a parking lot or driveway. If there is none, pull as far off the road as you can.
- Once stopped, turn off all lights except the hazards, set the parking brake and take your foot off the brake pedal, so drivers behind you are not misled into following your tail lights.
