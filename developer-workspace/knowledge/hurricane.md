# Hurricane safety

Source: https://www.weather.gov/safety/hurricane-action
Source: https://www.weather.gov/media/safety/hurricane_safety.pdf

## Before the storm
- Find out whether you live in a storm surge or evacuation zone, and plan where you would go.
- Buy supplies before hurricane season starts rather than in the rush before a storm.
- Protect windows: permanent storm shutters are best; otherwise use pre-cut 5/8-inch exterior-grade or marine plywood ready to fit.
- Follow your local National Weather Service office and local emergency management, and keep NOAA Weather Radio or another radio handy.

## Evacuation
If you live in a storm surge risk area, be ready to leave, give yourself enough time to pack, and tell family or friends where you are going. If local officials order an evacuation, leave immediately.

## Sheltering in place
If you are not told to evacuate, stay indoors in a small interior room, closet or hallway on the lowest floor, with as many walls as possible between you and the outside. Keep away from windows, skylights and glass doors.

## The eye of the storm
If the eye passes over you, there will be a short calm. Do not go outside: the winds return quickly at hurricane strength from the opposite direction.

## After the storm
Floodwater and downed power lines remain dangerous after the wind drops. Follow the flood safety guidance (Turn Around, Don't Drown) and stay away from fallen lines.
