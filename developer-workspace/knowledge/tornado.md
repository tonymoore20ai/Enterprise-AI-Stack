# Tornado safety

Source: https://www.weather.gov/safety/tornado-during

## Staying informed
When severe storms are forecast, keep track of tornado watches and warnings through NOAA Weather Radio, local TV or radio, or phone alerts, so you can act quickly.

## What to do when a tornado warning is issued
- At home: go to a basement, storm shelter or safe room. If there is none, use a small interior room on the lowest floor, away from windows. Bring pets if there is time.
- In a mobile home: mobile homes are not safe in a tornado. Leave right away for a sturdy building or shelter.
- At work or school: follow the tornado drill and go calmly but quickly to the designated shelter area. Avoid windows and large open rooms such as cafeterias, gyms and auditoriums.
- Outdoors: get inside a sturdy building immediately. Sheds, tents, storage units and mobile homes do not protect you.
- In a vehicle: driving during a tornado is not safe, but if you can, drive to the nearest sturdy shelter. If you cannot reach one, either stay in the car, lie down and cover your head, or get out and lie in a low area such as a ditch, covering your head.
