# Driving in rain and on wet roads

Source: https://www.dot.nv.gov/safety/rainy-day-driving-safety-tips

## Before you drive in rain
- Drive in heavy rain only when necessary, and allow extra time for the trip.
- Defrost the windows before setting off, and keep them clear while driving.
- Dry wet shoes on the car's mats so they do not slip off the pedals.
- Wear your seatbelt.

## Driving on wet roads
- Slow down: posted speed limits are set for dry, normal conditions.
- Turn on your headlights so other drivers can see you, and keep your wipers on.
- Leave more space than usual between your vehicle and the one ahead.
- Turn off cruise control to lower the risk of hydroplaning.
- Brake earlier and more gently than usual, and take turns more slowly.
- Watch closely for other vehicles and for pedestrians.

## Braking and skids on wet pavement
- Do not slam on the brakes. With anti-lock brakes (ABS), press firmly and steadily; without ABS, pump the brakes gently if needed.
- If the car starts to skid, steer in the direction of the slide and gradually ease your foot off the accelerator.

## When visibility is poor or roads flood
- If you cannot see the road or other traffic clearly, pull over to a safe place and wait.
- Never try to cross running or flooded water on the road. Turn around and find another route.
