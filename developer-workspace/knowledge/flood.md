# Flood and flash flood safety

Source: https://www.weather.gov/media/twc/floodsafetytrifold.pdf
Source: https://www.weather.gov/ama/tadd

## Turn Around, Don't Drown
Never drive or walk into flooded roads or water of unknown depth, whether you are in a car or on foot. Turn around and find another route. More than half of all flood deaths happen to people who drive or walk into floodwater.

## How little water it takes
- About 6 inches of fast-moving water can knock an adult off their feet.
- About 12 inches of moving water can carry away a small car.
- About 18 to 24 inches of moving water can carry away larger vehicles, including SUVs, vans and trucks.
Floodwater can also hide washed-out roads, debris and downed power lines.

## Driving at night
It is much harder to judge water depth and whether the road beneath it is still intact in the dark. Treat every flooded stretch of road at night as impassable.

## Staying safe
- Stay informed through the National Weather Service (weather.gov), NOAA Weather Radio or local news when heavy rain is forecast.
- If you live in a flood-prone area, move to higher ground when flooding threatens, and leave immediately if officials order an evacuation.
- Keep children away from flooded areas, storm drains and fast-moving water.
